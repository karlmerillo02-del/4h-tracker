How to use this guide

  1. Measure the classroom. Use an indoor thermometer for temperature and a humidity meter for relative humidity. Follow the device instructions and let it settle in the occupied area, away from direct sun, windows, fans, air conditioners, and hot equipment. The linked DepEd guidance describes readings at 8 a.m., noon, and 2 p.m.; follow your school’s current instructions.
  2. Enter the measured values. Temperature is in °C and humidity is in %. Add an optional room name, then tap Get decision.
  3. Save what you need. Save each result to this device’s history. Export the history to CSV for a spreadsheet or delete it when it is no longer needed.
  4. Use the result with local guidance. Check learners and classroom conditions; this tool supports decisions and does not replace school or official guidance.

No thermometer or humidity meter? A weather app may show current outdoor conditions near you, but those are not classroom readings. Offline weather data may also be old. Do not enter outdoor values or guesses as room measurements. Ask your school to provide or lend an indoor thermometer and humidity meter. Until you can measure the room, follow your school’s and local authorities’ guidance.

    Your privacy

    This app does not send readings to a server.

    Readings stay on this screen unless you choose Save reading. Saved readings are stored only in this browser on this device and may be removed by clearing browser data or uninstalling the app.

    Use Export CSV if you need a separate copy. Treat exported files as sensitive and store them securely.
